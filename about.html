About

Jamie Park.

I'm a freelance designer based in Fort Lee, NJ. I work mostly with small brands and independent businesses — helping them figure out how they look, how they sound, and how they show up online.

Outside of work, I'm usually watching a series, trying out a new recipe, or figuring out what Cloud feels like eating today.

Cloud is a four-year-old Bichon Frise who has, over time, become the main subject of most of my photos, the reason I read ingredient labels at pet stores, and my best friend.

I write about the things I actually care about — food, design, everyday life, and Cloud. Nothing too serious. Just things worth sharing.
